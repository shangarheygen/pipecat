- Added `SegmentedAudioClassifier` in `pipecat.classifiers.segmented_audio_classifier`, the base class for classifiers that answer questions about one clip of audio. Pass the clip as a `ClassifierAudio` (16-bit mono PCM and its sample rate) with `audio=` to `ask()`, `yes_no()`, `choice()` or `score()`. Each classifier says how long a clip can be (`min_audio_secs`, `max_audio_secs`) and the `sample_rate` it's sent at; a clip at another rate is resampled.
